### Case Study 1: TOY FACTORY
**Titolo:** Hidden quality defects exposed
**Tech:** Computer Vision, Real-time Analysis

**Risultato:** Eliminated subjective QA decisions. Defect escape rate reduced to near-zero.

---

### Case Study 2: BAE SYSTEMS
**Titolo:** Real-time process visibility
**Tech:** Process Analytics, Optimisation Engine

Energy and resource consumption varied wildly with no clear explanation. We created real-time visibility into process efficiency, showing exactly where waste was occurring and how to eliminate it.

**Risultato:** Operators now see optimal scheduling in real-time. Resource waste identified and eliminated.

---

### Case Study 3: ANYBOTICS
**Titolo:** Remote equipment monitoring
**Tech:** Remote Inspection, Anomaly Detection

Critical equipment in hostile environments couldn't be inspected safely. We enabled remote visibility into gauges, liquid levels, and thermal anomalies — without putting people at risk.

**Risultato:** 24/7 visibility in hazardous areas. Issues caught before they become failures.

---

### Case Study 4: AYLA NETWORKS
**Titolo:** Predicting failures before they happen
**Tech:** Predictive Analytics, Root Cause Analysis

Device reboots were causing customer complaints but happening without warning. We built a system that sees the patterns leading to failure — 12 hours before it happens.

**Risultato:** 12-hour advance warning with >85% accuracy. Proactive intervention now possible.

---

### Case Study 5: MEDICAL IMAGING SILICON
**Titolo:** Finding defects before assembly
**Tech:** Defect Detection, Quality Analytics

Faulty silicon was being assembled into expensive equipment, discovered only at final test. We built visibility into wafer quality at the slice level — catching defects before they become costly.

**Risultato:** 100% of defective slices identified before assembly. Rework costs eliminated.
